Operating instructions

Start: center G/H, release J, then switch power on. Wait for closing to finish and keep controls neutral for 0.1 seconds. The water command starts off. A startup-held input is consumed; release/center, then make a fresh command.

Normal: tap J for water on/off. G increases the level; H decreases it. Center or reverse the rocker to make another adjustment. Blue shows the running level; white shows the paused level. While paused, the maximum lamp blinks blue/off or blue/white.

Set Max: hold J for 1.5 seconds, then release. G/H changes the maximum from 10% to 100%; one blue/white lamp shows it. Tap J to save and return. Running water holds its opening while editing; an OFF command continues closing. Saving selects the nearest available level under the new cap.

Flush: in Set Max, release J and hold it again for 1.5 seconds. This saves the cap and opens fully. A fresh J press returns immediately to the previous Normal on/off state. G/H movement is tracked but cannot change settings in Flush. Wait for any earlier closing command to finish before starting the Flush hold.

Tap or hold: a tap is shorter than 0.5 seconds. Releasing between 0.5 and 1.5 seconds cancels the gesture. Hold feedback starts at 0.5 seconds with an inward white fill. Flush uses an outward white ripple.

Fault recovery: select No active fault cause, release J and center G/H, then hold J for 3 seconds or use Reset fault. Keep G/H centered during the hold; moving it cancels reset. Wait for closing, then release J and center G/H for 0.1 seconds. Water remains off. Removing the cause or cycling power alone does not acknowledge a fault. A continuously held J faults after 30 seconds.

Settings and latched faults persist through the simulated power switch but reset on page reload. Power loss stops the modeled motor at its current position; it does not close the valve. The switch models controller power. A constant-hot machine connection may stay powered with the ignition off. Buttons remain operable during power-off and recovery so held-input cases can be tested.

Keyboard: focus J and use Space or Enter. Reduced-motion preferences replace flashing with steady markers. Full operator guide · Fault codes
